# Doctor Project

This directory holds Shlomo's personal medical information and related work — records, lab
results, appointment notes, and health tracking. It lives inside the Shlomo-Afanasev repo as a
separate, clearly-scoped project because a dedicated repo could not be created in this session
(the GitHub integration here is limited to already-authorized repos and cannot create new ones).

## Handling medical information

- Treat everything in this directory as sensitive personal health information. Don't summarize,
  export, or repeat its contents outside of direct responses to Shlomo.
- When extracting or organizing records (lab results, prescriptions, visit summaries), preserve
  exact values, dates, and units — do not round, approximate, or "clean up" clinical data.
- Flag ambiguous or concerning values plainly rather than softening them, but do not provide
  diagnoses or medical advice — surface information clearly and suggest discussing it with a
  doctor when relevant.
- Prefer the existing `docx`, `pdf`, and `xlsx` skills (installed at the repo root, so already
  available here) for reading and producing medical documents.
